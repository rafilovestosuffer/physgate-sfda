## 1. Claims (reordered by C39 after the 2026-09-13 external audit; supersedes the Session-0 order)

1. **Physics gating of SFDA pseudo-labels, judged by realised false acceptance on real healthy bearings**
   (C65): which gate designs are safe (envelope-domain kinematic screens) and which are not (raw-spectrum ROI
   rules), how sparse the safe ones are, and whether gating helps adaptation at all (M2/M3). Claim the
   *evaluation of gating*, never the superiority of our detector (C40, C65).
2. **External validation of that gate against Smith & Randall's per-record diagnosability grades,
   under domain shift.** Delta vs `predictive-maintenance-mcp`: theirs is rule-based and in-domain.
3. **The first leakage-safe evaluation of SFDA for bearing diagnosis**: Vieira et al.'s (MSSP 2026)
   bearing-wise methodology, cited as the methodology source, applied to the cross-machine
   source-free setting they explicitly scope out, measuring how much published SFDA gain survives.

**Standing wording rule (halt condition, C50):** no novelty sentence in any repo file or manuscript
without (a) a named nearest published neighbour and (b) a one-sentence delta.
We do **not** claim a novel backbone. No Transformers, no attention modules, no KANs.

## 2. Label spaces `[FROZEN]`

- `L3 = {Normal, Inner, Outer}` — mandatory wherever PU is involved. PU has no ball class.
- `L4 = L3 + {Ball/Roller}` — CWRU / JNU / HUST only.
- `L4c = L3 + {Ball, Cage}` — **UORED only** (C43). FTF is a deciding family on L4c only, and only where
  `feasibility.py` certifies it; everywhere else C22 stands.
- Compound faults excluded from main tables, reported separately as an out-of-label-space stress
  test. Never folded into single-fault classes.

## 3. Splitting policy `[FROZEN]`

1. Bearing-wise. No physical bearing on both sides of any task. Enforced by a load-time assertion
   that hard-fails, itself tested with a deliberately leaky config.
2. Healthy signals split bearing-wise too. **CWRU has effectively one healthy configuration and
   cannot be split this way** — stated as a limitation, never hidden.
3. Fixed-length windows; no overlap across a split or record boundary; within-record overlap declared.
4. Secondary table: every method re-run under the conventional condition-wise ("leaky") split.
   The delta between tables is a deliverable, not a diagnostic.

## 5. Metrics `[FROZEN]`

Oracle-gap closure (headline); leaky-vs-clean delta; pseudo-label noise transition matrix;
diagnosability-stratified gate precision/recall; gate ROC with realised vs nominal FAR; A7 Jaccard
at record level with reported power; per-class always with ball/roller separate; macro-F1 with
accuracy; ECE and NLL; `expected_false_accepts`; `N_records`.

Mean ± std over 5 seeds, never best-of. Wilcoxon signed-rank pairwise; Friedman + Nemenyi across
many methods. **A method that wins on the mean but fails the test is reported as not significant.**

## 6. Ablations `[FROZEN]`

A1 gate on/off; A2 band selection; A3 SES vs log-envelope; A4 rejected samples discarded vs
entropy-only; A5 α sweep → gate ROC; A6 harmonic family depth;
**A7 overlap with confidence filtering (Jaccard) — highest priority, run earliest.**

Ablation ladder L0–L8 as defined in `PLAN.md` §2.7.

- **H3** gate/representation coherence.
- **H4** must beat order-tracking alone; CWRU nominal-RPM confound declared (biases H4 *in our favour*).
- **H5** sideband necessity.
- **H6** the 6203 lock — DRS on/off.
- **H7** mechanism: frequency-position rule vs cyclostationarity-order test. **Unblocked (C49):** the
  "EAGLE-style rule" is specified from Bio-SFDA §4.2 (ROI band-energy/SNR predicates on the STFT at
  BPFO/BPFI/BSF, activation-rate-bounded thresholds). Run on PU and, once geometry is verified, UORED.
- **H8** (C46) specificity out of label space: on UORED cage records the gate rejects the O/I/B
  families at a rate materially above its rejection rate on true O/I/B records. Zero GPU.
- **H9** (C59) memorisation: under Vieira's controlled leakage design, KNEOS-HC shows smaller bearing-level
  leakage inflation than plain SES. Reported either way.
