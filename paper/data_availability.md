# Data availability

All four datasets are public: CWRU Bearing Data Center; Paderborn University KAt-DataCenter (Lessmeier et al. 2016, citation conditions apply); Jiangnan University; HUST bearing (Mendeley Data doi:10.17632/cbv7jyx4p9, CC BY 4.0).
The Smith & Randall (2015) per-record stratification is derived from predictive-maintenance-mcp (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
