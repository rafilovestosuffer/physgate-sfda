\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
Paderborn carries the full analysis; HUST replicates only the direction and size of the SDALR gap and changes bearing size together with specimen. These are cross-condition, bearing-disjoint evaluations within each rig; no cross-machine claim is made. HUST's unpublished pitch diameter excludes it from kinematic analyses.

Two SFDA methods supply adaptation evidence, and SHOT \cite{liang2020shot} is SDALR's own base, so they are not independent families; NRC \cite{yang2021nrc} and AaD \cite{yang2022aad} require evaluation before any method-general conclusion. The first SHOT implementation had an indexing defect (Supplementary~S6); only the corrected run is reported. The networks use 2048-sample windows, which poorly resolve the harmonic structure used by the gates. No SFDA experiment on full-record order-domain inputs was run; the forest comparison also changes duration, aggregation and class weighting.

Three seeds were used on the two folds, but only one on each of ten random Paderborn splits and six HUST splits. Reused bearings and shared models limit independence. The nominal tests assume null sign symmetry, and their intervals describe the fixed pool under the stated resampling model. They do not establish a population effect or equivalence. The two fold jobs trained different source checkpoints, so effects are never compared across jobs and their oracle decomposition is omitted.

A bearing-disjoint split changes the damage signature, manufacturer and session with the specimen. Paderborn's outer-race pool mixes fatigue pitting and particle-caused plastic deformation; all six real inner-race specimens have fatigue pitting \cite{lessmeier2016,iso15243}. Each bearing has its own mounting and session, which are not crossed independently. The within-bearing contrast is descriptive across different trained models. The same-condition and source-expansion controls use forests, and source expansion also increases training volume; neither establishes a causal mechanism for SDALR. Only four source bearings per class can be studied with these pools.

The tested envelope pipeline certifies four real-damage specimens on more than one recording at adaptation conditions. Damage extent is associated with coverage but not experimentally isolated. Ball faults are absent from the Paderborn real-damage label space. Low false acceptance on six healthy specimens does not establish safety, and target-rig characterisation uses known healthy status. The raw-spectrum rule is a simplified implementation, not the full Bio-SFDA method. No gate-override baseline or independent held-out target-record evaluation was run; gains concentrated on certified records therefore do not establish transfer beyond transductive filtering.

The source-only, within-bearing, spillover, purity and interval analyses are post-hoc. Source-diversity and kinematic experiments had decisions specified before their own runs but followed earlier results and are treated as exploratory. The frozen protocol and all run artifacts are preserved; Supplementaries~S1, S6 and~S7 document protocol changes, the implementation audit and the reporting checklist. A planned class-conditional identity remedy was cut for budget before its outcomes were observed; its effectiveness relative to source expansion is unknown.
